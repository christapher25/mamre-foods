2026-10-02 - Price history (owner decision, Doc 1 s4.3): a PriceDefault or PriceOverride whose effective_from is today or earlier cannot have its unit_price_cents changed; a change is a new row with a later effective_from, and PriceHistoryError is raised if the new date is not later than the row now in effect. Rows dated in the future may be corrected in place. Override note and is_active may still change on a row in effect. Every change keeps its AuditLog row. "Today" is timezone.localdate() (P-7 pending) and services accept a today argument for tests.
