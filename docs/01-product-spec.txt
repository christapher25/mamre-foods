## 1. Purpose and Scope
==============================================================================

### 1.1 Business context

Mamre Foods is a small US-based food business. It makes half-cooked chapathi, packs it in packets (a standard packet is 12 chapathis, a custom packet any number from 1 to 200), and sells to restaurants, shops, retail customers and caterers. Some customers pay once a month, some pay in parts. The owner sells, keeps the books and reviews them. All money is in US dollars. Tax handling is out of scope.

### 1.2 Goals of version 1

1. Make a bill on the handheld in under a minute and print it.
2. Keep every customer's balance correct to the cent, including monthly credit and part-payments.
3. Record returns, production damage, material purchases and expenses, so version 2 can calculate cost and profit from real history.
4. Export every record to files.

### 1.3 Version 1 and version 2

|  | Version 1 (first 1 to 2 months) | Version 2 (built while version 1 is in use) |
|---|---|---|
| Where it runs | One Android handheld. No server, no network. All data is stored on the phone (A-24). | A server and database, the Android app, and a laptop. |
| Who uses it | One user, the owner, who is both salesman and admin (A-25). | Owner on the laptop, salesmen on handhelds, each with a login. |
| Included | PIN lock. Sales area: bills, payments, returns, today's bills. Admin area: sales list and void, customers and balances, prices and price rules, material purchases, other expenses, production damage, returns list, settings. Bill printing. Export to files. | Laptop dashboard. Monthly dashboard and profit analytics. Costing screens. PDF and Excel reports. Sync between devices. Separate salesman login. Automatic backups. Import of the version 1 export. Month lock. |
| Data safety | No automatic backup (A-26). Data is entered by hand and can be re-entered. | Server backups. |

### 1.4 Non-goals for version 1

A server, a laptop dashboard, the monthly dashboard and profit reports, the Costing screen, PDF or Excel reports, sync, automatic backups, tax or VAT, payroll, a customer portal, online payments, loyalty schemes, Arabic text on bills, multiple devices, and anything not written in this document.

### 1.5 Success criteria

- A bill is made and printed with no network at all.
- A customer's balance on the screen equals the sum of their bills, credits and payments.
- A confirmed bill, payment, purchase or expense can never be edited or deleted, only voided or reversed.
- The export contains every record, and its row counts match the app.
- A release app starts empty (reference data only) and contains no demo code or demo data.

## 3. Products
==============================================================================

Two products at launch, both sold half-cooked. Adding a new product must be a simple Admin form with no code change.

| Product | Description | Standard packet |
|---|---|---|
| Mamre Fresh Chapathi | Half-cooked chapathi, no preservative | 12 chapathis (editable) |
| Mamre Chapathi | Half-cooked chapathi with potassium sorbate as preservative | 12 chapathis (editable) |

- Product fields: name, code, standard packet size, yield per kg of wheat (32), active flag. Packing is a material (section 9), not a product field.
- The sales unit is the packet. A packet is standard (12 chapathis, A-19) or custom (1 to 200 chapathis, available for every customer type). Each invoice line stores its packets and the chapathis per packet, so one invoice can mix sizes of the same product on separate lines (A-1).
- A new product can be sold as soon as it has prices. Its cost shows as incomplete until a recipe exists.

## 4. Customers and Pricing
==============================================================================

### 4.1 Customer types and customers

Customer types: Restaurant, Shop, Retail and Catering. A saved customer has: name, location (area or branch), type, phone, address, payment mode (Cash or Credit), a corporate account switch, optional opening balance, notes, active flag.

- Name plus location must be unique, ignoring case and extra spaces, so two shops with the same name in different places never clash. Location is required for Restaurant, Shop and Catering; optional for a new Retail name; and always required when the name already exists. Screens show 'Name - Location'.
- Corporate account (for chains such as supermarkets): no balance of any kind is shown on the bill or anywhere in the Sales area. The balance is still tracked and visible in the Admin area.
- A walk-in sale is an anonymous Retail sale with no ledger; it follows the Retail price list and the Retail price-edit setting, and must be paid in full at the time of sale.
- Customers are created and edited in the Admin area only (A-13) and apply immediately.

### 4.2 Price resolution order

1. An active override price for that customer and product (set in the Admin area, used for special or discounted prices).
2. Otherwise the default price for that customer type and product.
3. Otherwise no price: the product cannot be sold. The app shows 'No price set - contact admin'. Never default to zero.

Price of a custom packet (unless changed on the line where allowed) = list price per standard packet x N chapathis / standard packet size, rounded half up, integer arithmetic only. Examples: 250 cents per standard packet of 12 gives 208 cents for 10 chapathis and 188 cents for 9 (a half rounds up); 230 cents gives 192 cents for 10 chapathis.

### 4.3 Price rules

- Prices carry an effective-from date. History is kept. A new price for a type and product needs a later effective date than the current one.
- Each bill line stores a snapshot of the unit price charged and the list price at that time. Later price changes never alter existing bills.
- Price editing by customer type. Each customer type has a switch 'Salesman can edit price', set in the Admin area and change-logged. Default: ON for Retail and Catering, OFF for Restaurant and Shop. When ON, the Sales area lets the price per packet be changed on any line (above $0 and at most 10 times the list price). When OFF the price is read-only. The rule is enforced in the business logic, not only on the screen.
- A changed price is stored with the list price and a flag price_overridden. The Admin area shows 'List 2.60, charged 2.47' on the bill and has a 'Price changed' filter. The bill shows only the charged price.
- Discounts for a regular customer are done as an override price.

>> [NOTE]
> Risk. With one trusted owner the risk of under-charging is small. It matters when a second salesman is added in version 2. A minimum price per product is an open question (P-11).

## 5. Billing
==============================================================================

### 5.1 Flow (Sales area)

1. Choose the customer type (Restaurant, Shop, Retail, Catering), then the customer, or Walk-in under Retail.
2. Add products. For each line choose Standard (12) or a Custom packet (1 to 200 chapathis) and the number of packets. Prices and the total appear automatically; the price can be changed only where the type allows it.
3. Payment step: amount received now (may be 0 for credit customers) and method.
4. Confirm. The bill is saved on the phone and printed.

### 5.2 Bill number

Format MAM-<DEVICE>-<SEQ>, for example MAM-W1-0042. The device code is a setting (default W1). The sequence is stored on the phone, increases by one, is saved in the same step as the bill, and is never reused. Keeping the code lets version 2 merge bills from several devices without clashes.

### 5.3 Bill layout (32 columns, modelled on the parent company's bill)

- Header: business name, address lines and phone from Settings (a missing value prints '(address / phone pending)'), then a rule line.
- Lines in this order: Bill No, Date, Time, Salesman (the owner's name from Settings), Customer (the name, then the location on the next line, indented). The customer type is never printed.
- Item table with columns Item Desc, Qty, Price, Amt. Each item is its name in capitals with the chapathis per packet (for example MAMRE CHAPATHI 12NOS), then a line with packets, price per packet and amount, right-aligned. No dollar sign anywhere on a bill; the total line reads TOTAL (USD).
- Payment block: a PAID line for money taken now (with the method) and a CREDIT line for the unpaid part.
- Normal customers: Balance after, and for credit customers a THIS MONTH summary (brought forward, invoiced, credits, each payment with date, amount and method, total due).
- Corporate accounts: no balance of any kind (no Balance after, THIS MONTH, Brought forward or TOTAL DUE). The bill ends with 'Received by' and 'Sign and stamp' so the store can sign and stamp it.
- Footer text from Settings. A reprint adds DUPLICATE COPY. A voided bill shows VOID and keeps its number.
- Payment receipts (a payment with no new bill) use the same header and plain numbers.
- Item codes, a logo and Arabic text are not printed in version 1: they need the printer's own library and real product codes.

### 5.4 Rules

- A confirmed bill cannot be edited. Mistakes are fixed by a void in the Admin area (reason required; the number stays and shows VOID) or by a return credit.
- Reprinting is allowed from Today's bills and is marked DUPLICATE COPY.
- A failed print never loses the bill. It is saved first, then printed, and can be reprinted.
- When a payment is confirmed, a receipt prints. A payment with no new bill (a customer pays an old balance) also prints a receipt (A-16).

## 6. Payments and Customer Ledger
==============================================================================

### 6.1 Payment methods and customer modes

- Methods accepted: Cash, Zelle, Check, Card, Other (with a note) (A-3).
- Cash customers pay at the time of sale. Credit customers may leave a balance and are billed per calendar month in the business time zone (P-7).
- This applies equally to restaurants, shops and named retail customers.

### 6.2 Partial payments and allocation

- A customer may pay any amount. Several part-payments in a month are normal.
- Each payment is applied to the oldest unpaid invoice first, spilling into the next as needed. Allocation rows are stored. The server performs allocation when records sync.
- An overpayment becomes credit on account (negative balance), shown as such (A-4).

### 6.3 Balance

>> [RULE]
> Customer balance = opening balance + non-void invoices - credits from returns - payments. It is always calculated from records. It is never a typed or editable number.

### 6.4 Month summary and month lock

For any customer and month the system can show: opening balance, invoiced, credits, each payment (date, amount, method), and closing balance. In version 2 the Admin can lock a month after review. A locked month accepts no back-dated entries; corrections go into the current month (A-5). Version 1 has no month lock.

### 6.5 Worked example (used as a test case)

A credit restaurant starts October with a $0.00 balance.

| Date | Event | Amount | Balance after |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oct 3 | Invoice 1 | $120.00 | $120.00 |
| Oct 10 | Invoice 2 | $90.00 | $210.00 |
| Oct 12 | Payment, cash | -$150.00 | $60.00 |
| Oct 20 | Invoice 3 | $60.00 | $120.00 |

Allocation of the $150 payment: $120.00 clears Invoice 1 completely; $30.00 goes to Invoice 2, leaving $60.00 owing on it. Closing balance $120.00 = $60.00 (Invoice 2) + $60.00 (Invoice 3).

==============================================================================
## 7. Returns and Damage
==============================================================================

### 7.1 Customer returns (recorded by worker)

Fields: customer, product, packets (with their size), reason (Damaged, Quality complaint, Wrong item, Expired, Other), optional linked invoice, and a resolution:

- Credit: reduces the customer's balance at the unit price on the linked invoice, or the customer's current price if no invoice is linked.
- Replacement: free replacement packets are handed over. The balance does not change. The replacement chapathis are used up like any others and are counted in material usage (section 9). Their cost is shown on the returns list for information only.

### 7.2 Production damage

Chapathi damaged during production is entered by the Admin in chapathis (date, product, chapathis, note) and is add-only (A-7). It counts in material usage, so it raises the cost of every packet sold. It appears on the returns and damage list but is not a customer return.

### 7.3 Handling of returned goods

Returned product is half-cooked and perishable, so it is not restocked; it is a write-off (A-6). Reports show returns and damage on their own lines.

## 8. Production and Stock
==============================================================================

Batch records and finished-goods stock are not part of v1 (A-8 withdrawn). The amount of each material used is calculated from what was sold, replaced and damaged (section 9), and the Admin tracks raw-material stock from purchases. A negative closing stock shows a warning, because it usually means a purchase was not entered. It never blocks a sale.

## 9. Costing
==============================================================================

>> [VERSION 1 AND VERSION 2]
> In version 1 the Costing screen and the Dashboard are hidden (version 2). The app still stores every input (purchases, recipe, wastage, expenses, damage), so version 2 can calculate cost and profit from real history. The rules below are final.

### 9.1 Principles

- Cost is calculated by the system from purchases, recipe, sales and damage. It is never typed in by hand, and workers never see it.
- Both products share one list of materials, so one bag of wheat serves both.
- Missing inputs are flagged as incomplete. A missing value is never silently treated as zero.

### 9.2 Recipe basis: per 1 kg of whole wheat flour

| Material | Quantity per 1 kg wheat | Mamre Fresh Chapathi | Mamre Chapathi |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whole wheat flour | 1 kg | Yes | Yes |
| Oil | 80 ml | Yes | Yes |
| Sugar | 20 g | Yes | Yes |
| Salt | 15 g | Yes | Yes |
| Baking powder | 2 g | Yes | Yes |
| Potassium sorbate | PENDING (P-2), in grams | No | Yes |
| Yield | 32 chapathis per kg (editable) | Yes | Yes |
| Packing | 1 piece per packet, bought like any material | Yes | Yes |

### 9.3 Quantities per chapathi and per standard packet of 12

| Material | Per chapathi | Standard packet (12) |
|---|---|---|
| Whole wheat flour | 31.25 g | 375 g |
| Oil | 2.5 ml | 30 ml |
| Sugar | 0.625 g | 7.5 g |
| Salt | 0.46875 g | 5.625 g |
| Baking powder | 0.0625 g | 0.75 g |
| Potassium sorbate (Mamre Chapathi only) | PENDING (P-2) | PENDING (P-2) |
| Packing | n/a | 1 piece |

A custom packet of N chapathis uses N times the per-chapathi quantities and one packing piece.

### 9.4 Purchases and stock

- The Admin enters each purchase: material, date, quantity and unit, total paid, a note. A helper turns bags x kg per bag into kilograms. Purchases are add-only. A mistake is corrected by a reversing entry (negative quantity and amount, a required reason, linked to the original). An entry can be reversed once and a reversal cannot be reversed (A-18).
- Opening stock is entered once per material when records start; later months carry forward.
- Price per unit = weighted average = (value of opening stock + value of purchases) / (opening quantity + quantity bought) (A-10).
- Closing stock = opening stock + bought - used. A negative result shows a warning. A material that was used but has no price makes the result INCOMPLETE.

### 9.5 Usage

>> [RULE]
> Used (per material) = (chapathis invoiced + replacement chapathis + production-damaged chapathis) x recipe quantity per chapathi x (1 + wastage).
> Chapathis invoiced = packets x chapathis per packet, over non-void invoices, gross: return credits are not subtracted, because returned goods were already made. Wastage is a setting, default 2%, adjustable from 0% to 5%, and applies to ingredients only. Potassium sorbate is counted only for Mamre Chapathi.
> Packing used = packets invoiced + replacement packets, with no wastage. Damaged chapathis are lost before packing, so they use materials but no packing (A-20, A-22).

### 9.6 Calculation rules

1. Cost consumed per material = used x price per unit. Direct expense for the month = cost consumed over all ingredients + packing consumed.
2. Direct cost per packet, per product = (ingredient cost per chapathi x chapathis in the packet) + packing price per piece.
3. Indirect cost per chapathi for a month = total indirect expenses / net chapathis sold, both products together, equal per chapathi (A-9). Net chapathis = invoiced chapathis - chapathis credited through returns.
4. Full cost per packet = direct cost per packet + indirect cost per chapathi x chapathis in the packet.

### 9.7 Worked examples (illustrative prices, NOT real)

Stock example. In a month 12,000 chapathis are invoiced, replaced or damaged. Wastage is 2%.

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Wheat needed before wastage (12,000 x 31.25 g) | 375 kg |
| Wheat used with 2% wastage | 382.5 kg |
| Bought: 400 kg for $440.00 | $1.10 per kg |

Cost of a standard packet of 12 chapathis (Mamre Fresh Chapathi, before wastage). Invented prices: wheat $1.10/kg, oil $4.00/L, sugar $1.00/kg, salt $0.80/kg, baking powder $4.00/kg; monthly indirect expenses $900 over 72,000 net chapathis ($0.0125 each).

## 10. Expenses
==============================================================================

### 10.1 Direct expenses

Direct expense is the cost of the materials used (wheat, oil, sugar, salt, baking powder, potassium sorbate) plus packing, calculated from purchases and usage as described in section 9. It replaces the earlier recipe-times-batch method (A-11 withdrawn).

### 10.2 Indirect expenses

Categories at launch: Electricity, Water, Machine, Labour, Maintenance, Delivery charge, Fuel, Other. An entry has: date, category, amount, description. The Admin logs expenses at first; worker logging of fuel and delivery is a later version. Machine cost is entered as a monthly amount (rent, lease or an amortised share) (A-12).

Expense entries are add-only. A mistake is corrected by a reversing entry with a reason, as for purchases (A-18). The Admin can add categories.

## 11. Lists, Export and Reports
==============================================================================

### 11.1 Version 1: lists and export

Version 1 has no dashboards, charts or profit reports. The Admin area shows lists with search and filters: Sales (month, customer, status, Price changed), Customers with their month summary, Balances with ageing (current, 30+ days, 60+ days), Returns and damage, and Purchases and other expenses.

Export to files (Admin area): one CSV file per table (customers, customer types, products, prices, bills, bill items, payments, returns, materials, purchases, expenses, production damage, settings without the PIN, change log) plus a manifest with the data version, export time and row counts. UTF-8, header row, UUIDs, money in cents. The Owner shares the files with the phone's share menu or saves them to a folder. There is no import in version 1; version 2 imports these files.

### 11.2 Version 2: reports (parked)

1. Sales summary: packets, chapathis and revenue by product and by customer type; invoiced total, credits, net sales.
2. Collections by method and the outstanding balance at month end.
3. Direct expense and gross profit.
4. Materials: a monthly table per material (opening stock, bought, used, closing stock, average price, cost consumed), with warnings.
5. Indirect expenses by category and net profit.
6. Customer balances with ageing, returns and damage, cost per chapathi and per standard packet, price changes, and activity per salesman.

>> [PROFIT DEFINITIONS (VERSION 2)]
> Net sales = non-void bills - return credits.
> Direct expense = cost consumed of all materials + packing consumed (section 9).
> Gross profit = net sales - direct expense.
> Net profit = gross profit - indirect expenses.
> Cash collected = payments received in the month (a separate cash view).
> Production damage, wastage and replacement packets are already inside direct expense, so they are shown for information and never subtracted a second time.
