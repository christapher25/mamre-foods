MAMRE FOODS - HOW TO INSTALL THE APP
====================================

Read this whole page before you start. Follow the steps in order.

1. BEFORE YOU START
-------------------
You need three things:
- the handheld phone,
- the link you were sent,
- about 15 minutes.

Choose now a username and a password. Write them on paper. The password must
be at least 6 characters. Keep the paper in a safe place.
The app warns you about this. It says:
"If you forget the admin password, the only way back in is to clear the
app's data, which deletes everything."

2. INSTALL
----------
1. Open the link you were sent, on the handheld.
2. Download the file. When the phone says it can download, tap OK / Download.
3. Open the downloaded file.
4. If the phone asks to allow installs from this source, turn it on once.
   Then go back.
5. The phone shows the app name "Mamre Foods" and a button "Install".
   Tap "Install". The words can differ a little on each phone.
6. When it says the app is installed, tap "Open".

3. CREATE THE ADMIN ACCOUNT
---------------------------
The first time, the app shows "Create the admin account" and this line:
"The admin sees everything. Choose a password of at least 6 characters
and enter it twice."
Fill in these boxes:
- "Your name (printed on bills)": 2 to 40 characters.
- "Username": 3 to 20 letters, digits, dots or underscores. Capital letters
  do not matter.
- "Password": at least 6 characters.
- "Password again": the same password once more.
Then tap "Create account".
You do this once only. Every later start shows "Log in" with the boxes
"Username" and "Password" and the button "Log in".

4. SET UP YOUR BUSINESS
-----------------------
The next screen is "Set up your business". It says:
"This prints on your bills. You can change it later in Admin, Settings."
Fill in these boxes:
- "Business name"
- "Address (up to 4 lines)"
- "Phone"
- "Bill footer"
Tap "Save and continue".
You may tap "Skip for now" and enter the details later.

5. THE HOME SCREEN AND "Finish setup"
-------------------------------------
The Home screen shows these buttons: "New invoice", "Record payment",
"Return", "Today's invoices" and "Admin area".
While something is missing, Home also shows a card "Finish setup".
The card can list:
- "Business details" (only when the address or the phone is empty),
- "Prices:" followed by a number and the word "missing".
The card goes away by itself when nothing is missing.

6. SET THE PRICES
-----------------
1. On the card, tap the line that starts with "Prices:". It opens "Prices"
   in the Admin area.
2. Under "Default prices", a product that has no price shows "No price".
3. Tap "Set new price".
4. Type the price in "New price per packet".
5. Leave "Effective from" as it is.
6. Tap "Save price".
7. Do the same for every product, until the card no longer lists prices.
A product with no price cannot be sold. The bill screen then says
"No price set - contact admin".

7. ADD YOUR CUSTOMERS AND SALESMEN
----------------------------------
You can do this now or later. It is not needed before your first bill.
Customers: tap "Admin area", then "Customers", then "Add customer".
Fill in the boxes and tap "Save customer".
Salesmen: tap "Admin area", then "More", then "Settings", then "Users".
Tap "Add salesman" and fill in the boxes "Name (printed on bills)",
"Username", "Password" and "Password again". Tap "Add salesman".
A salesman can make bills, take payments and make returns. A salesman
cannot open the Admin area.
To go back to selling from the Admin area: tap "More", then "Sales area".

8. YOUR FIRST BILL
------------------
1. On Home, tap "New invoice".
2. Choose the customer. Customers are grouped by type: tap a type, then the
   customer. For a person who is not a customer, tap "Walk-in sale".
3. Enter the number of packets for each product.
4. Tap "Continue".
5. On "Payment", type the money you received in
   "Amount received now", or tap "Full".
6. Tap "Continue".
7. Tap "Confirm invoice". The box "Confirm invoice?" appears. Tap "Confirm".
The bill opens on the screen. Tap "Print" to print it on the handheld's
printer.
If the screen says "Printer not ready. The bill is saved. Tap Reprint." then
tap "Reprint".
When you are finished, tap "Done".
A confirmed bill cannot be changed. The app says it too:
"A confirmed invoice cannot be edited."

9. EVERY DAY
------------
When someone else will use the phone, tap the small lock sign at the top of
the Home screen. It logs you out. You can also do it in "Admin area", "More",
"Settings", "Log out".
Once a week, make a safety copy: tap "Admin area", then "More", then
"Settings", then "Export data", then "Export and share".

10. THE RULES TO REMEMBER
-------------------------
NEVER UNINSTALL THE APP. Never clear its data. That deletes every bill,
customer, price and payment, and they cannot be brought back.
Updates are installed over the old app. See the page "How to update the app".
Keep the paper with your username and password.
