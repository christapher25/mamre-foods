MAMRE FOODS - HOW TO UPDATE THE APP
====================================

Read this whole page before you start. It takes about five minutes.

THE ONE RULE
------------
NEVER UNINSTALL THE APP.
If you uninstall it, ALL your bills, customers, prices and payments are deleted
from the phone and cannot be brought back. An update does not need an uninstall.
You install the new version OVER the old one and everything stays.

BEFORE YOU UPDATE (as a precaution)
-----------------------------------
1. Open the app and enter your PIN.
2. On the Home screen tap "Admin area", then "More", then "Settings", then
   "Export data". (If the Home screen shows a card that says "Open Export",
   you can tap that instead.)
3. Tap "Export and share". Send the files to your own email or Google Drive,
   or save them to a USB stick.
   The screen shows "Last export prepared" with today's date when it worked.
This is only a safety copy. The update itself keeps your data.

THE UPDATE
----------
1. Open the new link that you were sent, on the phone that has Mamre Foods.
   [screenshot: out\C2\guide-update-01.png]
2. Download the file. When the phone says it can download, tap OK / Download.
3. Open the downloaded file.
   If the phone asks "Allow from this source?", turn it on once. You did this
   when you first installed the app.
4. The phone shows the app name "Mamre Foods" and a button "Update"
   (on some phones it says "Install"; it is the same thing).
   Tap "Update".
   [screenshot: out\C2\guide-update-02.png]
   Do NOT tap "Uninstall" or "Delete" on any screen.
5. When it says the app is installed, tap "Open".
6. The app asks for your PIN ("PIN" and the button "Open"). Enter the PIN
   you always use. It must NOT ask you to choose a new PIN. Your customers,
   prices and bills are all still there.
   [screenshot: out\C2\guide-update-03.png]

IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
------------------------
- The phone says "App not installed" or "Newer version already installed":
  you are trying to put an older version over a newer one. Do nothing,
  keep the app you have, and ask for the newest link.
- The app asks you to choose a NEW PIN, or your data is missing: STOP.
  Do not uninstall, do not clear data, do not install again.
  Call for help and keep the safety copy from "Before you update".
- You cannot find the file you downloaded: open your phone's "Downloads"
  folder or "Files" app and look for the newest "mamre" file.

REMEMBER: update = install over the old app. Never uninstall.
